Available from October 1st 2026, Bob On-Premises deploys the AI inference layer on your own Red Hat OpenShift cluster. The IDE experience is unchanged; only the backend moves inside your perimeter. Air-gap compatible.

This is the answer to the question I hear from every bank, insurer, and government customer: *"Can Bob see our source code?"* With On-Premises: no. Your RPG programs, COBOL, and business logic never leave the network.

The architecture made possible by the [July 2026 decoupled design](/ai-bob/2026/07/01/ibm-bob-premium-packages-architecture.html): IDE layer and inference layer are now fully separable. The IBM i AppMod lab below runs identically against a self-hosted backend.
